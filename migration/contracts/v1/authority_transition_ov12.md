# OV12 authority transition

Go is the migration authority for diagnostics, evidence health, problem graph,
privacy retention, and support export.

The Android app may remain non-buildable through OV12 under the amended roadmap.
Android debug screens are not authoritative. System → Diagnostics is the future
canonical UX surface and must consume the Go diagnostics contract during OV13.

OV12 does not alter cleanup mutation authority from OV08 and does not add new
unattended destructive automation.
