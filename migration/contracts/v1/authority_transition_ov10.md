# OV10 authority transition

Cleanup intelligence is owned by Go. Kotlin business-policy implementations for duplicate grouping, residue classification, recommendation safety labels, and cleanup rule matching are no longer migration authority. Android may render Go outputs but cannot relabel bare paths as verified, safe, duplicate, residue, or rule-approved.

Go mutation authority from OV08 remains the only destructive authority; OV10 only supplies proof-bearing candidates and plan intelligence to that authority.
